# [GAME TITLE] — Edition 1: Runner Edition
### Edisi 1: Edisi Lari

**Tagline:** Every problem is a point. Keep running. / Setiap masalah itu poin. Terus lari.

**Alternative titles:** Kejar Gajian (Chasing Payday) · Lari Terus (Keep Running) · Tagihan Runner (Bill Runner) · Kewarasan Run · Gajian Rush

**Players:** 1 to 6 (solo score attack, or a 2 to 6 player race) · **Playtime:** 60 to 90 minutes · **Age:** 17+ · **Languages:** English first, then Bahasa Indonesia, on every card, gate, and HUD label

**Assumptions I made:** This is a real-time game (app or web), not a table game, because the controls are left, right, and jump. Issues and Good News are the only sources of points. Your existing 160-card library is kept, but each card becomes a gate you run through, not a card you draw on a turn.

## 2. Controls

Only three inputs exist in the game. Left arrow moves your runner one lane to the left. Right arrow moves it one lane to the right. Up arrow or Space makes it jump. There is no crouch, slide, hold, or menu input during a run. On touch screens, the same three actions are swipe left, swipe right, and swipe up. Each player needs their own key set or device at a shared table. Six players need six controllers or phones.

Each input does exactly one thing, and the game buffers an input for 0.1 seconds, so a jump pressed just before landing still counts.

---

## 3. Setup

Choose the number of Shifts. Eight Shifts is the default, and four is a quick game. Every player picks a runner and an input device. All runners start in the middle lane on the same seeded track, so everyone meets the same Issues, Obstacles, and Gates in the same order. Every runner begins with Kewarasan 10 and 0 Points. Shuffle the seven Gate decks separately and place them face down. Shift 1 starts in Zone 1.

## 4. Rules

### 4.1 The Run

Runners move forward automatically and never stop, so the only decisions are which lane to be in and when to jump. Speed starts at 1.0 and rises by 0.05 every 10 seconds, capped at 2.0. The speed bands have names: Santai (Relaxed) below 1.4, Kejar Deadline (Chasing the Deadline) from 1.4 to 1.7, and Panik (Panic) above 1.7. The faster the band, the less time you have to react, and the more Issues cluster together.

### 4.2 Points

Points come from two sources only: Issues and Good News. Points never go down. Money effects from the old cards, bills, and fines all become Issues, so they add points. Your sanity is the cost of getting them.

Small Issues (Notifikasi, Notification) are worth 10 points. Medium Issues (Tagihan, Bill) are worth 25 points. Big Issues (Deadline) are worth 50 points. Good News (Kabar Baik) is worth 150 points. Bonus (Bonus Akhir Tahun, Year-End Bonus) is worth 200 points. Every point you earn is multiplied by your current Combo.

### 4.3 Kewarasan

Kewarasan (Sanity) starts at 10 and cannot go above 10 or below 0. Lose 1 Kewarasan when you hit a standard Obstacle, and lose 2 when you are caught by a Chaser or hit a Sudden Meeting. After any hit you are invincible for 1.5 seconds, and your runner flashes, so one obstacle cannot drain you twice. You gain Kewarasan from Self-Care pickups (+1), from the night's rest between Shifts (+3), and from some Gate cards.

When Kewarasan reaches 0, you enter Burnout. Your run for this Shift ends, and your points stay where they are.

### 4.4 Combo (Tumpukan Masalah, Pile of Problems)

Every five Issues you collect in a row without taking damage raise your Combo multiplier by 0.5, up to a maximum of ×3. Taking any damage resets the multiplier to ×1. The combo is visible on every runner's HUD, so players can see who is on a streak and what it is worth.

### 4.5 Items: Lempar Tagihan (Throw a Bill)

When your Combo reaches ×2, you earn one Lempar Tagihan item. Using it sends a Tagihan obstacle into the lane of the nearest runner ahead of you. A runner who hits it loses 1 Kewarasan. The bill also counts as a Small Issue for them, so they still gain 10 points. The item therefore changes how you race, but it never takes points from anyone.

### 4.6 Chasers (Penagih, Debt Collector)

A Chaser appears behind your runner, moving at your speed plus 0.2. It spawns when you collect a Paylater pickup and 20 seconds later, or at random in Zone 1 and Zone 4. It is always visible for at least three seconds before it reaches you. If you are in its lane when it arrives, it tags you for 2 Kewarasan and then disappears. You can escape it by switching lanes or jumping its obstacles, and it never causes a crash without warning.

### 4.7 Gates and Cards

A Gate is a glowing archway across all three lanes, and it appears about once every 20 seconds. You trigger it by running through it in any lane. Its card pops up for two seconds, and the effect applies immediately. Each card is used once, and when a deck runs out, its discard pile is shuffled to form a new one. Gates in Zone 7 draw mostly from Politik, and Gates in other Zones draw from a mix based on the Zone.

### 4.8 Shifts and Burnout

A Shift lasts a maximum of six minutes. It ends early when every runner is in Burnout. At the start of each Shift, every runner gains 3 Kewarasan from the night's rest, up to a maximum of 10, and any runner still in Burnout returns from the Klinik Burnout checkpoint at 3 Kewarasan. Between Shifts, there is a 90-second Rapat Pagi (Morning Meeting). The leaderboard is shown during the meeting, and nothing can be done on it except watch.

### 4.9 Winning

The runner with the most total points after the last Shift wins. If points are tied, the runner with the most Kewarasan at the end of the final Shift wins. If that is also tied, the runner with fewer Burnouts wins. In a solo game, the goal is to beat your own best total.

An optional Survival variant replaces points with survival. The last runner above 0 Kewarasan wins. This variant is for players who want the original elimination feel, and it is not the default.

## 5. Track: The Seven Zones

The street loops through seven Zones in order. Each Zone is about 400 meters of track, which takes about 45 seconds at speed 1.0. Every Zone is a satirical version of a publicly known type of institution, and each one has its own spawn table and Zone Issue.

| # | Zone (EN / ID) | Based on (type) | Visual theme | Zone Issue (points) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Kos-kosan Pinggir Jalan / Roadside Boarding House | Developer (landlord) | Alley, leaking pipes, drying laundry | Landlord Notice: Medium Issue (25) |
| 2 | Stasiun KRL / Commuter Station | Transit operator | Platform crowd, train horn | Packed Carriage: Chaser spawn, collect extra Small Issues |
| 3 | Kantor Open Space / Open-Plan Office | Platform, developer | Cubicles, glowing monitors | Overtime Email: Big Issue (50) |
| 4 | Mal / Shopping Mall | Retail chain | Escalators, sale banners | Impulse Buy: Medium Issue (25) and a Chaser spawn |
| 5 | Rumah Sakit / Hospital | Hospital group | Long corridors, waiting-room chairs | Admission Deposit: Big Issue (50) |
| 6 | Kampus / Campus | Private university | Lecture hall, course-fee posters | Tuition Due: Big Issue (50) |
| 7 | Balai Kota / City Hall | Public agencies | Stacked offices, queue numbers | Policy Gate Zone: Gates appear every 12 seconds |

Shift 1 starts in Zone 1, and each Shift starts in the Zone after the last Zone of the previous Shift, so every Shift gets a different sequence.

## 9. Balance Notes

Points only go up, so the leaderboard always shows a race to collect, and sanity is the only real cost. This is the central satire of the design: problems raise your score, and they also wear you down. The trade-off between taking risks for Issues and protecting your Kewarasan is what the game is about.

The Combo can snowball. A runner on a ×3 streak collects points fast, so the design needs a way back. Runners who burn out are restored to 3 Kewarasan at the start of every Shift, and Good News appears on a fixed timer rather than only for the leader. Lempar Tagihan sends points to the victim as well, so the item cannot push a runner out of contention on its own.

Sanity swings are bounded. Obstacles cost 1 or 2, Chasers cost 2, Self-Care gives 1, and Gate cards stay between −3 and +2. No single hit can end a run from full health, so a run ends after a run of decisions, not a single unlucky one.

## 10. Playtest Checklist

1. Does one runner snowball on a ×3 Combo in the first 90 seconds, and does the rest of the table have a way back?
2. Can every obstacle layout be cleared from all three lanes at the top speed band? Test each layout.
3. Does the median player burn out before two minutes into the first Shift? If so, lower the obstacle rate or increase the invincibility window.
4. Do players stop collecting Issues out of fear? If so, the risk is too punishing or the points are too small.
5. Is Good News rare enough to feel like a gift, and not background noise?
6. Can a player see a Chaser coming for at least three seconds, and does a catch feel fair?
7. Do the Balai Kota Gates land equally across political viewpoints? Read the Politik cards aloud to players with different views and ask whether each side feels mocked.
8. Does Lempar Tagihan create frustration loops, such as one runner being hit over and over?
9. Do players understand that points only go up, and that Kewarasan is the real cost?
10. Does a full eight-Shift game finish in 60 to 90 minutes, including the Rapat Pagi meetings and the first-time teaching?

## 11. Content Note

The game follows the content guardrails from the Edition 1 design brief. Political content targets systems, incentives, and institutions, and it does not name living politicians, celebrities, or private individuals. Institutions appear only as types, such as a developer, a transit operator, a hospital group, or a university, and the satire relies on publicly known business models and public policies. No card invents a crime or scandal and presents it as fact.

Dark jokes deal with debt, mortality, and anxiety, and they do not glamorize self-harm, suicide, eating disorders, or drug use. Burnout and anxiety are satirized, not promoted. The Chaser is a cartoon pursuer. A catch costs Kewarasan, and no card or visual depicts violence against a person. There are no slurs, and no card attacks anyone for their race, religion, ethnicity, gender, sexuality, disability, or nationality. There is no sexual content, and no character is a minor. Satire is aimed upward at systems and incentives, not downward at people for who they are.
